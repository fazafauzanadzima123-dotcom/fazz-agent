What Orbit can do today

Chat & voice

Streaming answers from Gemini. Tap the mic to speak; Orbit can read replies aloud.

Open apps

"Open YouTube" launches installed apps by name.

Calendar

Reads upcoming events and prepares new ones in your calendar app. You tap Save.

Contacts

Finds a person by name, only after you approve each lookup.

Phone status

Battery, network, storage, date and time, timers, and sharing text.

Memory you control

Remembers only what you approve. View or delete anything in Settings.

Install on your phone

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.
  3. Tap Install, then open Orbit.
  4. Optional: in Orbit, open Settings → Test connection to confirm the AI is reachable.

Privacy & safety

No AI keys in the app

The Gemini key stays on the server. The app talks to it over HTTPS only.

You approve sensitive actions

Calendar and contact data are shared only after a confirmation dialog. Permissions are asked when first needed.

Fixed, safe tools

The AI can only use a reviewed list of tools. It can never run code on your phone.

Stored on your phone

Chats and memories stay on the device. Passwords, PINs and card numbers are never saved.

Honest limits

Early preview. This is a debug build that isn't on Google Play, so Android will warn you before installing it. There's no wake word and no background listening yet; Orbit listens only while the mic panel is open. Web search depends on Gemini quota and may be unavailable. Each device is limited to a daily number of requests.
